Reports

Members reported from a campaign's chat (docs/COMMS.md, "Reporting"). The reported message's text is kept with the report.

    Takeover requests

    A co-GM of a campaign whose owner was suspended, asking to be made its owner. Approve hands the campaign over.

      Users

      Suspending an account signs it out everywhere, keeps it out (with the reason shown at sign-in), and shuts every campaign it owns until it is unsuspended or the campaign is transferred.

      Use Grant Site VIP to give a player the gold name and avatar effect throughout every campaign.

        Every campaign

        A site admin is not a game master. To look inside a campaign you are not in, Join it as a player (and leave from its People panel afterwards); Delete removes a campaign and everything in it.
