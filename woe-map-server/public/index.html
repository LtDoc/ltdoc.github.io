02 — The campaign

Think beyond
the next battle.

A successful offensive needs more than an army. Bring the battlefield, the home front and the negotiating table into the same campaign.

I

Command the front

Move infantry, armour, ships and aircraft. Dig trenches, hold provinces and rebuild broken rail links. Optional fog of war makes reconnaissance matter.

II

Keep a nation running

Turn resources into supplies, equipment and new forces. Develop towns, invest in research and keep the supply lines open when the front moves.

III

Give diplomacy a voice

Trade, make alliances and negotiate peace. Keep the conversation close with private groups, user mentions, replies, pinned messages and dice rolls.

03 — The people

One table. Different perspectives.

The game master sets the stage.
The players decide what happens next.

For game masters

Build the world.
Keep the story moving.

Set the rules, place starting forces and invite your players. Manage turns, treasuries and announcements, then view the campaign from a nation's perspective when you need to check what it can see.

Campaign setup · Turn control · Marker and drawing history

For players

Take command.
Make your choices count.

Lead your nation's forces, production, research and diplomacy within the campaign's rules. Coordinate with allies, read your turn report and ready up when your orders are in.

National economy · Military orders · Private conversations

04 — Getting started

Your first turn starts here.

Starting a new world or joining an existing one?
You only need an account to begin.

  1. 01

    Make an account

    Choose a username and a display name so the table knows who you are.

  2. 02

    Create or join

    Create a campaign as its GM, or enter the join code shared by your game master.

  3. 03

    Set the stage

    The GM chooses Earth or imports an Azgaar map, sets the rules, assigns nations and places the starting forces.

  4. 04

    Write the next chapter

    Issue orders, talk to your neighbours and ready up. The GM advances the turn.
