      Each side rolls the die and adds attack or defend. The loser loses base + margin × extra; the winner loses the winner's loss. Ties cost both half the base.

      Map

      Large images are shrunk in your browser to at most 8192 px on the longest side and re-encoded before upload, so phones are not sent 40 MB files.

      Or use the Earth

      Vector coastlines, lakes and rivers from Natural Earth (1:50m, public domain), drawn in WebGL so they stay sharp at any zoom. Replaces any uploaded image. The measure tool then reports true great-circle kilometres.

      Editable borders

      Turn a year's borders into locked area drawings, one per country, so you can reshape them like any drawing (unlock, then grab the line). Countries are simplified to at most 300 points each.
