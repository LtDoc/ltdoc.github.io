      General

      Used by the measure tool. Example: if 100 px on your map is 25 km, enter 0.25 and "km".

      Combat

      Each side rolls the die and adds attack or defend. The loser loses base + margin × extra; the winner loses the winner's loss. Ties cost both half the base. A side only causes strength loss when its penetration meets or exceeds the target's Armour Class.

      Default attack, defend, AP, range, movement, Armour Class and penetration per unit type.

      Map

      Import an Azgaar map

      Upload Azgaar's Full JSON export. It imports the map, nations, borders and settlements into an empty campaign.

      Upload an image

      Large images are shrunk in your browser to at most 8192 px on the longest side and re-encoded before upload, so phones are not sent 40 MB files.

      Or use the Earth

      Vector coastlines, lakes and rivers from Natural Earth (1:50m, public domain), drawn in WebGL so they stay sharp at any zoom. Replaces any uploaded image. The measure tool then reports true great-circle kilometres.

      Editable borders

      Turn a year's borders into locked area drawings, one per country, so you can reshape them like any drawing (unlock, then grab the line). Countries are simplified to at most 300 points each.

      While the game runs players only plan trenches. Engineers and pioneers dig a section of a planned trench each turn (and reinforce a dug one), finished at End turn; sappers blow up a section of an enemy trench in the battle phase.

      Fog of war

      On the Earth map sight goes by province: a province is in sight when it is your own, when one of your units or places stands in it, or when its centre is within their range. On uploaded maps it goes by distance. An attacker is revealed to the side it attacks until End turn. Check what a nation sees with 👁 View as player.
